# BIHAR 360 — The Digital Atlas of Bihar

> An immersive digital atlas celebrating Bihar — its 38 districts, history, heritage, culture, food, festivals, people, languages, arts, and places.

## About

**BIHAR 360** is a digital cultural atlas designed to make Bihar discoverable through stories, places, people, traditions, and living culture.

Rather than presenting Bihar as a conventional tourism or administrative website, the project explores the state through an editorial and interactive experience.

### Explore Bihar

- 🗺️ **38 Districts** — Discover the identity and stories of Bihar's districts
- 🏛️ **Heritage** — Explore historical places and cultural heritage
- 🍲 **Food** — Discover regional cuisine and culinary traditions
- 🎨 **Arts & Crafts** — Explore Bihar's living artistic traditions
- 🎉 **Festivals** — Experience festivals and cultural celebrations
- 👤 **People** — Discover notable personalities and contributors
- 🌿 **Nature & Places** — Explore landscapes and destinations
- 🕰️ **History** — Travel through Bihar's historical timeline
- 🎵 **Music** — Experience Bihar's musical traditions
- 🧭 **Travel** — Discover places through curated journeys

## Project Goals

BIHAR 360 aims to:

1. Present Bihar through authentic cultural storytelling.
2. Make all 38 districts discoverable.
3. Connect places with their history, culture, food, people, and traditions.
4. Create an accessible and responsive experience across devices.
5. Preserve cultural context while making the experience useful for modern users.

## Design Principles

- Cultural authenticity
- Visual storytelling
- Accessibility
- Responsive design
- Performance
- Source attribution
- Accurate geographic and historical information
- Respectful representation of Bihar's diverse cultures and languages
